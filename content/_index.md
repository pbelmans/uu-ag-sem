title: Algebraic Geometry Seminar @ Utrecht University
---

This page lists the talks in the Algebraic Geometry Seminar at Utrecht University.
The math calendar for the Utrecht Geometry Centre can be found
[here](https://utrechtgeometrycentre.nl/calendar/).

The seminar is organized by [Pieter Belmans](https://pbelmans.ncag.info),
[Yuki Mizuno](https://yuki-mizuno728.github.io/homepage/) and
[Vanja Zuliani](https://sites.google.com/view/vanjazulianimath/). It was previously
organized by [Soumya Sankar](https://sites.google.com/site/soumya3sankar/),
[Pim Spelier](https://sites.google.com/view/pim-spelier/),
[Sara Mehidi](https://smehidi.perso.math.cnrs.fr/) and
[Woonam Lim](https://sites.google.com/view/woonamlim/). If you have any questions
about the seminar, please contact one of the organizers.
